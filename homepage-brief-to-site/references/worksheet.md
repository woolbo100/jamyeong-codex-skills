Use this worksheet as the intake for homepage creation. Adapt labels to the user's domain, but preserve the information goals.

## Quick Instructions For The User

Ask the user to answer what they can. Empty answers are allowed. The goal is to collect enough signal to make a specific homepage, not to create homework.
