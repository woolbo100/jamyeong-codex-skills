# Funding Style Templates

Read before recommending or applying a funding page style.

## A. 문제공감-해결책 설득형

Best for broad beginner offers, ebooks, templates, and VOD classes. Lead with the painful current state, explain why common attempts fail, then introduce the offer as a practical shortcut.

Visual tone: high-contrast headlines, before/after blocks, checklists, simple diagrams, practical proof.

## B. 창업자 스토리/미션형

Best for creator-led brands, communities, coaching, and identity-based launches. Build trust through the founder's before/after, mission, values, and why this offer exists now.

Visual tone: portrait, journal-like sections, dramatic chapter breaks, mission statements, warm but authoritative typography.

## C. 후기·성과 근거 집중형

Best when strong real reviews, funding results, student outcomes, case studies, or community activity exist. Put proof near the top, then explain the mechanism behind it.

Visual tone: review cards, screenshot mosaics, number highlights, evidence labels, source notes.

## D. 커리큘럼/로드맵 정보형

Best for courses, cohort programs, and complex knowledge products. Make the contents, order, time investment, deliverables, and learner journey extremely clear.

Visual tone: module timeline, roadmap, table cards, progress steps, learning outcome blocks.

## E. 얼리버드/혜택 전환형

Best for short funding windows, preorder offers, launch discounts, limited bonuses, or reward tiers. Only use deadlines, quantities, or discounts when verified.

Visual tone: countdown-like section design, tier comparison, bonus stack, CTA blocks, urgency badges.

## F. 프리미엄 코칭/고단가형

Best for mentoring, consulting, mastermind, or high-ticket programs. Reduce anxiety with qualification, process, support scope, fit/not-fit filters, and application flow.

Visual tone: restrained premium layout, founder authority, process diagrams, case-study blocks, application CTA.

## G. 커뮤니티 합류/정체성형

Best for memberships, communities, accountability groups, and challenge programs. Sell belonging, environment, rhythm, and identity transformation.

Visual tone: group activity visuals, principles, member journey, rituals, community benefits, join-us CTA.

## Recommendation Rules

- Beginner digital product: A or D
- Creator-led knowledge brand: B or G
- Strong proof available: C
- Deadline or reward tiers central to the launch: E
- High-ticket coaching: F
- Broad funding launch with little proof: A plus D
