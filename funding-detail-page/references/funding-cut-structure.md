# Funding Cut Structure

Use this to build the image sequence. Adjust labels to the selected cut count, but keep the persuasion order intact.

## 8-Cut Teaser

1. Hook and desired future
2. Problem and failed attempts
3. New opportunity or belief shift
4. Creator/founder credibility
5. Offer method or curriculum overview
6. Reward/benefit stack
7. FAQ, policy, and risk reduction
8. CTA and deadline/next step

## 15-Cut Standard Funding Page

1. Hero: item name, core promise, funding identity
2. Backer pain: current frustration
3. Why existing solutions fail
4. Opportunity: what becomes possible
5. Founder story: why this offer exists
6. Method: unique framework or roadmap
7. Curriculum/modules: what is inside
8. Deliverables/reward stack
9. Who this is for / not for
10. Proof: reviews, case studies, numbers, screenshots if verified
11. Experience: how the backer will use it
12. Reward tiers, early-bird, bonuses, or price logic
13. FAQ and objections
14. Delivery, refund, cautions, and policy
15. Final CTA: join, back, apply, or reserve

## 20-Cut High-Consideration Launch

Use the 15-cut structure and expand:

- add one cut for market shift or trend
- add one cut for detailed founder credibility
- add one cut for module-by-module roadmap
- add one cut for case study narrative
- add one cut for application or onboarding process

## Flow Rules

- Put the buyer's problem before the seller's credentials.
- Use proof only after the desired transformation is clear.
- Explain reward tiers before urgency.
- Put policy and cautions before the final CTA.
- Do not use urgency unless a deadline, quantity, or price change is real.
