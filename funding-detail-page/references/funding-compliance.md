# Funding Compliance

Use this before publishing or generating final images.

## Claims That Need Proof

Mark as `확인 필요` unless the user provides source evidence:

- income, revenue, profit, sales, funding amount, ranking, student count, review count
- before/after transformation results
- success rate, completion rate, refund rate
- certifications, awards, press, expert status, licenses
- deadlines, limited quantities, price increases, early-bird inventory
- refund guarantees and satisfaction guarantees

## Risky Wording

Avoid or soften:

- `무조건`, `반드시`, `100%`, `보장`, `확실히`
- `누구나 월 1억`, `한 달 만에 성공`, `자동수익 완성`
- `No.1`, `업계 최고`, `검증 완료`, `전액 환불 보장`
- medical, legal, tax, investment, psychological, or career guarantees

Safer patterns:

- `이런 분께 적합합니다`
- `실행을 돕는 구조로 설계했습니다`
- `제공 자료 기준`
- `개인별 결과는 실행 환경과 상황에 따라 달라질 수 있습니다`
- `상세 정책 확인 필요`

## Required Policy Sections

For knowledge-commerce funding pages, include when relevant:

- 제공 방식: VOD, PDF, 이메일, 라이브, 커뮤니티, 1:1, 다운로드
- 제공 일정: 오픈일, 발송일, 라이브 일정, 수강 기간
- 환불/취소 정책: platform policy or seller policy
- 유의사항: 개인별 결과 차이, 저작권, 재배포 금지, 상담 범위
- 문의 방식: email, channel, form, platform message if provided

## Sensitive Categories

For money-making, investment, career change, tax, law, health, psychology, or education claims, add a cautious note and do not imply guaranteed outcomes.
