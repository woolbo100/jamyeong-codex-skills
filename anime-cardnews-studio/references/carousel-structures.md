# Carousel Structures

Choose one structure based on the user’s goal, then adapt it to the available card count. These are decision patterns, not fixed templates.

## Educational / how-to

1. Outcome-focused hook
2. Why the issue matters
3. Common mistake or misconception
4. Key principle
5. Steps or examples split across the necessary cards
6. Quick recap
7. Save/share/try CTA

Best when the reader should understand or apply something. Put the strongest practical insight early; do not delay all value until the final cards.

## Story / empathy

1. Tension or relatable moment
2. Situation and desire
3. Friction or failed attempt
4. Realization
5. Change or method
6. Result and meaning
7. Reflective or conversational CTA

Best for personal brands and emotional topics. Use the character’s changing expression and pose to carry the narrative.

## Promotional / offer

1. Desired result or painful problem
2. Who this is for
3. Cost of the current problem
4. Core solution and differentiator
5. Key benefits or components
6. Proof, process, or objection response only when supplied or verifiable
7. Offer details
8. One clear CTA

Never invent testimonials, scarcity, credentials, prices, or performance claims.

## Checklist / listicle

1. Specific numbered hook
2. Brief framing
3. One useful item per card, or two only when genuinely short
4. Summary/checklist view
5. Save/share CTA

Use visual variation across items while keeping the same grid and character lock.

## Mixed authority carousel

1. Sharp insight or contrarian hook
2. Audience problem
3. Explanation or framework
4. Practical example
5. Mini checklist or next action
6. Brand-aligned CTA

Best for an expert or instructor building trust without turning every post into a direct promotion.

## Sequence checks

- Each card should make the next swipe feel necessary.
- The cover promises only what the carousel delivers.
- Context comes before a solution, but value is not withheld too long.
- Examples follow principles, not before the reader understands the point.
- The CTA follows naturally from the content goal.
