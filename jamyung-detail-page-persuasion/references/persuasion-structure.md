# Mobile Detail Page Persuasion Structure

Use this reference when turning a draft ecommerce detail-page flow into a cut-by-cut mobile sequence.

## Why The Common 7-Step Flow Needs Adjustment

A simple flow such as intro, problem, solution, differentiator, review, FAQ, CTA is a good starting point, but it is often too compressed for mobile commerce. Buyers usually need three extra transitions:

- A fast first-screen promise before problem explanation.
- A product-specific proof section before social proof.
- Practical buying information before the final CTA.

Do not treat the 7-step flow as wrong. Treat it as the backbone, then expand or reorder it based on buyer anxiety.

## Better Mapping

| Original Section | Revised Mobile Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Intro / hook / benefit | Hero + result promise | Show product and outcome immediately. |
| Problem empathy | Problem or desire mirror | Use concrete buyer language, not abstract pain. |
| Solution + core review | Solution promise + first proof | Explain what changes and why it is believable. |
| Differentiator + ingredients | Benefit and proof cuts | Split into 2-4 cuts if dense. |
| Customer verification / B&A | Evidence cut | Use only verified reviews, photos, or data. |
| FAQ | Anxiety removal | Include use, delivery, size, caution, return. |
| Outro / CTA | Purchase reason | Recap benefit, offer, and next action. |

## Buyer Psychology Checklist

Before finalizing the cut order, check whether the sequence answers these questions:

1. What is this product and why should I stop scrolling?
2. Is this about my situation?
3. What problem or desire does it solve?
4. Why should I believe it?
5. What exactly do I receive?
6. How do I use it?
7. Is there any risk, caution, or inconvenience?
8. What should I do now?

If a cut does not answer one of these questions, merge or remove it.

## Mobile Copy Density

Per cut, aim for:

- One main headline.
- One supporting line.
- Up to three labels or bullets.
- One primary visual idea.

Avoid placing long paragraphs inside images. Move detailed explanations into separate cuts or product-page text below the image if the platform allows it.

## Cut Count Guidance

Use 6 cuts when:

- The product is familiar and low-risk.
- The main benefit is obvious.
- The page is for a quick ad landing or simple test.

Use 12 cuts when:

- The product needs normal marketplace persuasion.
- Product details, usage, options, and FAQ matter.
- The seller wants a standard Naver/Coupang-style mobile detail page.

Use 15 cuts when:

- The product is expensive, premium, regulated, new, or high-consideration.
- Ingredients, mechanism, comparison, reviews, or usage education are central.
- The buyer needs extra reassurance before purchase.

## Recommended 12-Cut Blueprint

| Cut | Role | Copy Direction | Visual Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | One strongest outcome or benefit | Product centered, large readable title |
| 2 | Problem | "혹시 이런 상황인가요?" | Checklist, buyer scene, pain points |
| 3 | Solution | "그래서 이 제품입니다" | Product + simple mechanism |
| 4 | Benefit 1 | Main functional value | Icon labels or close-up |
| 5 | Benefit 2 | Material, ingredient, formula, structure | Detail crop, diagram, comparison |
| 6 | Benefit 3 | Convenience, routine, emotional value | Lifestyle scene |
| 7 | Proof | Verified fact, review, comparison, or spec | Evidence cards; no fabricated data |
| 8 | Usage | When and how to use | Step-by-step or real-life scene |
| 9 | Fit / Comparison | Who it is for, why it fits | Checklist or comparison table |
| 10 | Components | Option, size, package, quantity | Flat lay, package, labels |
| 11 | FAQ / Caution | Remove last objections | Q&A blocks, delivery/care notices |
| 12 | CTA | Recap and purchase action | Benefit summary + button area |

## Rewrite Heuristics

- If the page starts with company history, move it after the hero or remove it.
- If reviews appear before product explanation, move them later.
- If benefits and ingredients are in one dense cut, split them.
- If FAQ is missing, add it before CTA.
- If CTA is weak, add a concrete reason to buy now without inventing scarcity.
- If the product has no verified proof, use neutral detail proof rather than fake authority.

## Compliance Notes

Use `확인 필요` for missing facts. Do not create:

- Fake review excerpts.
- Before/after claims without proof.
- Medical or functional claims without allowed evidence.
- Certifications or test labels without source material.
- Time-limited discounts unless the seller provides them.
