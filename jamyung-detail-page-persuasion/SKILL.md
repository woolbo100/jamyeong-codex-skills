# Jamyung School Detail Page Persuasion

## Purpose

Turn a rough ecommerce detail-page idea into a mobile-first persuasion sequence that Jamyung School instructors and students can reuse in class, assignments, client work, and product-page planning. The skill focuses on structure, buyer psychology, cut order, concise Korean sales copy, and safe claim handling. It does not replace product-photo production or legal review.

Do not add third-party community links, external branding, or unrelated promotional text to the output unless the user explicitly asks for it. Keep the response branded for Jamyung School usage.

## When To Use

Use this skill for requests such as:

- `상세페이지 구조 수정해줘`
- `이 상세페이지 흐름 괜찮아?`
- `자명스쿨용 상세페이지 설득 구조 만들어줘`
- `수강생 과제용 상세페이지 흐름 만들어줘`
- `이 상품 상세페이지를 자명스쿨 방식으로 진단해줘`
- `후킹/공감/CTA 구조를 더 잘 팔리게 바꿔줘`
- A screenshot or draft list of ecommerce detail-page sections is provided and the user asks whether it should be revised.

If the user asks to generate final image cuts, first produce a plan and ask for approval before image generation.

## Core Principle

Lead with the customer's decision path, not the seller's explanation. A strong mobile detail page should move in this order:

1. Stop the scroll with the result, benefit, or strongest visual.
2. Make the buyer feel understood through a concrete problem or desire.
3. Present the product as the simplest believable solution.
4. Prove the reason to believe with specific facts, details, comparisons, or evidence.
5. Reduce purchase anxiety with usage, options, delivery, cautions, and FAQ.
6. End with a clear purchase reason and CTA.

## Default Output

Unless the user requests a shorter audit, provide:

- Overall diagnosis of the current structure.
- Recommended revised persuasion sequence.
- Mobile-first cut plan, usually 12 cuts.
- Korean headline and subcopy direction for each cut.
- Image composition notes for each cut.
- Claim safety notes separating facts, assumptions, and confirmation-needed items.
- Student-friendly reasoning that explains why the order changed, without turning the answer into a lecture.

Read [references/persuasion-structure.md](references/persuasion-structure.md) when creating or revising a cut-by-cut plan.

## Intake Rules

Ask only for missing information that materially changes the structure. Prefer proceeding with clearly marked assumptions when the user wants speed.

Minimum useful inputs:

- Product name or category.
- Target buyer or buying situation, if known.
- Main benefit or problem solved.
- Verified facts that may be used as proof.
- Product photos only when visual placement or final image planning is requested.

Do not invent certifications, reviews, rankings, sales numbers, medical effects, test results, ingredients, origin, price, delivery promises, or measurable performance claims.

## Structural Diagnosis Rules

When reviewing an existing structure:

- Preserve what works before rewriting.
- Identify where the flow asks the buyer to believe too much too early.
- Move proof after the buyer understands the problem and solution.
- Move FAQ, shipping, cautions, and option details near the end unless they are the main buying trigger.
- Split dense sections into mobile-friendly cuts with one job per cut.
- Replace seller-centered claims with buyer-centered outcomes.
- Keep intro copy short; mobile buyers should understand the page within the first two screens.

## Recommended 12-Cut Flow

Use this as the default unless the product is very simple or high-consideration:

1. Hero: result, benefit, strongest product visual.
2. Problem empathy: buyer pain, desire, or hesitation.
3. Solution promise: what the product changes.
4. Core benefit 1: most important functional or emotional value.
5. Core benefit 2: ingredient, material, mechanism, or usability.
6. Core benefit 3: convenience, routine, or differentiated point.
7. Detail proof: close-up, composition, spec, or verified evidence.
8. Usage scene: how and when the buyer uses it.
9. Comparison or checklist: why this option fits better.
10. Options, components, size, or package information.
11. FAQ, cautions, delivery, care, or return guidance.
12. CTA: benefit recap, offer area, purchase action.

For a compact page, use 6 cuts: hero, problem, solution, proof, FAQ, CTA.
For explanation-heavy, premium, regulated, or expensive products, use 15 cuts by expanding proof, usage, comparison, and anxiety-removal sections.

## Output Style

Write in Korean unless the user asks otherwise. Keep copy mobile-first:

- Headlines: short, concrete, one message per cut.
- Subcopy: one or two lines.
- Image notes: practical enough for a designer or generator.
- Tone: direct, persuasive, calm, and suitable for Jamyung School students; avoid hype.

Recommended response sections:

1. `# 자명스쿨 상세페이지 설득 구조 진단`
2. `## 1. 현재 구조 판단`
3. `## 2. 수정하는 게 나은 이유`
4. `## 3. 추천 설득 흐름`
5. `## 4. 컷별 제작안`
6. `## 5. 확인 필요 정보`
7. `## 6. 준법/표현 주의`

For each cut, include:

- 목적
- 헤드라인
- 서브카피
- 이미지 구성
- 사용 가능한 근거
- 확인 필요

## Claim Safety

Mark unverified items as `확인 필요`. Avoid absolute or hard-to-prove expressions such as:

- `100%`
- `무조건`
- `완벽한`
- `부작용 없음`
- `최고`
- `1위`
- `인증 완료` unless documentation exists
- disease treatment, cure, or medical-effect wording

For food, cosmetics, health supplements, medical devices, baby products, and pet products, add a compliance note before production use.
